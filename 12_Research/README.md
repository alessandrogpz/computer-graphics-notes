# Research

Orientation in the field as a research discipline rather than a craft: who publishes,
where, what the landmark results are, and which questions are open. Written in October 2026
while choosing a capstone topic.

Graphics research concentrates in one flagship venue — ACM SIGGRAPH and SIGGRAPH Asia,
published in ACM Transactions on Graphics — with a handful of specialist conferences and a
strong industry track alongside academia. Since January 2026 the entire ACM Digital Library
is free to read, so the bottleneck is finding papers rather than reaching them.

Notes on individual papers belong beside these files, one per paper, as they get read.
